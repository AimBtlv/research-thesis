## 3. How Was compound_form_reading_table.csv Built?

This step, for each compound sign from osl.asl, searches for its reading (complete, attested) across four layers of sources:
- @v in osl.asl itself
- the Diri table (Step 2)
- OGSL
- the modern syllabary (Syllabary_CM)

instead of relying on the readings of its individual constituent signs.

The goal is to provide the final dataset (Step 4) with the compound as a whole word, rather than a random choice of a single component sign (which often does not correspond at all to how the word was actually pronounced).
The pipeline tries to answer the questions:
- When this sequence of signs was read as a single word, what word was it?
- How do we know this? (from which source, with what degree of confidence)

## Data Sources

| Source | Description |
|---|---|
| **osl.asl** | Every compound sign, its components, and its own reading from the `@v` field. |
| **diri_lexical_list.csv** *(Step 2)* | Digitised extract of the ancient Diri = (w)atru series, 4,084 lines. |
| **ogsl_sign_readings.json** | OGSL (Oracc Global Sign List), a cross-project consolidation of sign values drawn from ABZL, BAU, HZL, KWU, LAK, MZL, RSP, SLLHA and other historical catalogues. Parsed from `ogsl.zip`'s portal export: sign detail pages carry a `Values:` field, extracted as `{sign_name: [readings]}`, 2,170 signs. |
| **Syllabary_CM.csv** | Merged historical syllabary (CM + URUK2 + ADDITIONAL), 21,460 entries, 4,196 distinct sign names (Step 1). |

## Pipeline Overview

| Stage | Task | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1** | Parse `osl.asl`, identify every compound, its components, and any `@v` readings | **2,389** distinct compound forms |
| **Stage 2** | Normalise every compound's name (strip `\|...\|` pipes, fold Unicode subscripts to ASCII) | comparable key for the three name-matched sources |
| **Stage 3** | Resolve in priority order: `@v` → Diri → OGSL → Syllabary_CM | `ATTESTED_DIRECT`, tagged with the source that supplied it |
| **Stage 4** | For compounds matched by none of the four, search for the name as a sub-sequence inside a longer, independently attested name | `NESTED_IN_LONGER_FORM` |
| **Stage 5** | Synthesise a fallback reading by concatenating each component's own individual reading | `component_reading_inferred`: a guess, never a fact |

**Output: `compound_form_reading_table_v13.csv`**, 2,389 rows.
**Script:** `06_build_sign_catalogue_v13.py` (Steps 3 and 4 are built by one script).

**Why 2,389 and not 2,178 rows (version 11):** in version 11, a sign that has `@form` entries in osl.asl was written only through its forms; the sign itself got no row (217 Unicode signs were affected, e.g. KA, SUM, TE, GAN). Since version 12 the sign gets its own row before its forms, so the compound signs among them are now in the table too.

**Note:**
**ATTESTED_DIRECT** a status indicating that the compound has a real, fully attested reading found in at least one of the four sources (OSL / DIRI / OGSL / SYLLABARY_CM).

**NESTED_IN_LONGER_FORM** a status for a compound that has no reading of its own, but is documented only as part of a longer, separately attested sequence; that is, it has never been read as an independent word.

**NO_ATTESTED_READING** a compound with no reading in any of the four sources and not nested in a longer form.

**component_reading_inferred** a compound's guess, not a fact: a reading obtained by simply hyphenating the readings of each component of the compound separately. It is filled in regardless of the status above (2,221 rows) and should never be considered a real attested reading.

## Step-by-Step Description

### 1. Pass Through Four Datasets

1. **`@v` in `osl.asl`** tied directly to the entity, no name-matching risk. **1,481 compounds**.
2. **Diri** the ancient primary source itself, checked before any modern compilation. **43 compounds**.
3. **OGSL** a project which consolidates historical paper sign lists. **19 compounds**.
4. **Syllabary_CM** the broadest modern compilation. **69 compounds**.

Combined: **1,612 of 2,389 compounds** (67.5 %) have an attested whole-word reading.

|Status|Compounds|
|---|---|
|ATTESTED_DIRECT|1,612|
|NESTED_IN_LONGER_FORM|90|
|NO_ATTESTED_READING|687|
|Total|2,389|

### 2. Difference of `.` in Diri and `.` in osl.asl

The dot `.` in osl.asl means only one thing: "this sign is composed of several other signs." This is a structural fact about the writing form.
"Diri" in a strictly scientific sense is a different, linguistic statement: the reading of such a sign cannot be guessed simply by knowing the readings of its component parts.
The dot says nothing about whether the reading can be guessed or not.

**Can we verify this?** Only for compounds that are confirmed specifically through the Diri table (diri_lexical_list.csv). The rest are confirmed via @v within osl.asl (most likely, the editors of osl.asl also took these readings from Diri or similar lists, but osl.asl itself does not say where exactly: we have the reading but do not know its source).

### 3. Compound Categorization

Each compound is classified into exactly one of six mutually exclusive categories by the operator in its name (`.`, `×`, `&`, `+`, `mixed` (`.` together with `×` or `&`), `other`). Within each category the share of ATTESTED_DIRECT and the breakdown by source are calculated:

|Category|Compounds|ATTESTED_DIRECT|Share|OSL|DIRI|OGSL|SYLLABARY_CM|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|`.`|1,633|1,273|78.0 %|1,176|34|13|50|
|`×`|392|110|28.1 %|94|0|5|11|
|`mixed`|320|213|66.6 %|197|8|0|8|
|`&`|34|11|32.4 %|9|1|1|0|
|`+`|8|3|37.5 %|3|0|0|0|
|`other`|2|2|100 %|2|0|0|0|

`.` sequences are attested far more often than `×` / `&` compounds.
`×` / `&` compounds are more often a documented **graphic** form than an independently attested **spoken** word.
`×` has no overlap with Diri at all (0 of 110 attested readings).
`.` is not equal to Diri: the presence of a dot only means "a documented sequence of signs" and does not guarantee that the reading is unpredictable from the parts (only 34 of the 1,273 attested `.` readings come from Diri).

### 4. The "Other" Category, Fully Identified
- `3/4(GUR)`: a fraction of the capacity measure gur; OSL reading stored as `3(barig@v)|3(bariga)@v` (the `@v` inside the value is a parsing artefact and should be cleaned to 3(barig) / 3(bariga)).
- `OO`: read oo (OSL).

**Note:**
`|A.AB.BA|` (`NO_ATTESTED_READING`) has in `osl.asl` the note `@inote` "writing for Tiʾamat in Tiʾamat-bašti", but no `@v` value; the inferred reading is only `'u4-ab-ba`.
`@compoundonly` marks a shape that has no `@sign` block of its own in `osl.asl` and exists only inside other compounds (142 such declarations).

### 5. Not Attested Reading (Guessed Compounds, `component_reading_inferred`)

Each compound's components are read individually (their own first-listed reading) and hyphen-joined. Explicitly a guess, not an attested fact: diri writing exists precisely because a compound's real reading is not guaranteed to equal its components' concatenation.

## Output Description
**`compound_form_reading_table_v13.csv`** 2,389 rows

●	**`compound_form`** the compound's name, exactly as documented in `osl.asl`.
●	**`compound_form_normalized`** without pipes (|...|).
●	**`PhoneticVersion_Compound`** attested whole-word reading(s), pipe-separated; filled only for `ATTESTED_DIRECT`, empty otherwise.
●	**`reading_status`** `ATTESTED_DIRECT` / `NESTED_IN_LONGER_FORM` / `NO_ATTESTED_READING`.
●	**`reading_source`** `OSL` / `DIRI` / `OGSL` / `SYLLABARY_CM`, in priority order; empty otherwise.
●	**`nested_in_forms`** for `NESTED_IN_LONGER_FORM` rows, the longer attested name this compound is documented as part of. Semicolon-separated.
●	**`component_reading_inferred`** hyphen-joined concatenation of each component's own reading.
