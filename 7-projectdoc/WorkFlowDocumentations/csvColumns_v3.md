#### Column	Description

№|column|Column	Description
|:---|---|:---
1|	unicode_id|	Unique identifier derived from the Unicode representation of the sign. Primary index in the relational network structure. Assigned based on Lemma or Lemma + Sign Variant. Numbers are not used.
2|	sign_cuneiform|	Representation of the sign in cuneiform script (Unicode glyph).
3	sign_trlitScien	Scientific Transliteration (value) of the sign. The standard name used in academic sign lists (e.g. A, LUGAL, DIŠ).
4	sign_trlitPhonet(atf)	Phonetic Transliteration (value) of the sign as attested in the specific ATF text (Polyphony). This is the actual reading used by the scribe, not the standard unicodeTrLit from the reference table.
5	sign_translation	Semantic translation of the sign (to be filled manually or from a translation dictionary).
6	artifact_id	Tablet identifier corresponding to the P-number used in the CDLI database. Extracted from ATF metadata where P (id) is specified.
7	corpus_id	Identifier representing the textual corpus or genre. Format: string identifier (e.g. ADMIN, SCHOOL, LIT, LEGAL).
8	genre_name	Human-readable name of the genre/corpus. Format: string text (e.g. Administrative corpus, Lexical / school corpus).
9	provenance	Excavation site where the artifact was discovered. Source: CDLI website filter option.
10	archaeological_context	Administrative center associated with the excavation site. Excavation sites from the CDLI website are grouped into main administrative cities of the historical period.
11	period_index	Categorical period identifier. Format: string (e.g. Early Dynastic I). Source: CDLI website (artifacts_10055.csv).
12	period_dates	Numerical representation of the chronological period. Format: integer (approximate BCE date, e.g. 2600).
13	languages	Language of the tablet (e.g. Sumerian, Akkadian, Emesal). Extracted from ATF #atf: lang tag.
14	sign_type	Functional type of the sign in this specific context. Values: LOGOGRAM | SYLLABOGRAM | NUMERAL | DETERMINATIVE | UNKNOWN. Determined by original ATF token case and PhoneticsVersion data from the syllabary.
15	is_compound	Structural type of the sign. Values: COMPOUND | SIMPLE. Derived from allograph_type == Type_2 (not regex heuristic).
16	has_analogue_source	Abbreviations of analogue paper catalogues associated with this sign form (e.g. LAK; BAU). Parsed from signList_analogue. Empty if no analogue source is documented.
17	allograph_type	Digitization level of the allograph. Values: Type_1 (own Unicode code point) | Type_2 (compound, @useq) | Type_3 (analogue name only). Source: osl.asl via allograph_all pipeline.
18	allograph_form	The specific @form value from osl.asl representing this graphical variant (e.g. AŠ@c, LAK797, |A.BAD|).
19	graphic_variant_id	Stable identifier for the graphic variant of this sign (e.g. A_v1, DI_v2). v1 = canonical / most attested form. Generated by add_graphic_variant_id.py from allograph_all pipeline.
